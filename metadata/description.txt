GramMeter is a personal food log. Search the Catalog or drop a barcode onto the Weigh readout, tare the vessel, and record net grams against daily energy and macro gauges.

• Today shows energy, protein, carbs and fat versus Targets, and Stations: Weigh-In One, Weigh-In Two, Weigh-In Three and Tare
• Weigh reads a live barcode or a Manual barcode, then computes net grams from Tare (vessel) and Gross in g, oz or ml
• Portion presets store Slice, Cup and Tbsp values per product
• Assign weigh-in picks a Station and Eaten today or a Future day. Tare is eaten-only and remaps to Weigh-In Three when planning ahead
• Catalog searches Open Food Facts by name. A bundled local shelf fills in if the network is quiet
• Log lists eaten weigh-ins by Station, with Previous day and Next day. A weigh-in can be deleted
• Plan lists staged weigh-ins for the Next 14 days. Eat converts a planned item to eaten today
• Reserved holds products you intend to buy. Reserve from Weigh; Weigh on a reserved row loads it on the scale
• Targets edits Energy kcal, Protein g, Carbs g and Fat g. Skip on first launch writes factory gauges
• Contact on Targets opens the support page
• Sources on Today, Targets and the last onboarding page cite USDA Dietary Guidelines, NIH DRI/AMDR, Open Food Facts, FDA and WHO

Resolved products are cached on the device. Nutrition data comes from Open Food Facts, a public database. GramMeter is a personal food log, not medical advice.
